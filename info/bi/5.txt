🔷 What is K-Means Clustering?
K-means Clustering is an unsupervised algorithm that:


divides data into K groups


groups similar points together



🔷 What Does “K” Mean?
K
= number of clusters.
Example:
K = 2
means:
divide data into 2 groups.

🔷 Main Idea of K-Means
Algorithm works by:


selecting centroids


assigning nearby points


recalculating centroids


repeating until stable



🔷 Important Terms
TermMeaningClusterGroup of similar pointsCentroidCenter of clusterIterationRepeating processDistanceSimilarity measure

🔷 K-Means Working Steps
5

🔶 Step 1: Choose K
Example:
K = 2

🔶 Step 2: Select Random Centroids
Initial cluster centers selected randomly.

🔶 Step 3: Calculate Distance
Distance formula:
d=(x2−x1)2+(y2−y1)2d = \sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2 + (y_2-y_1)^2}d=(x2​−x1​)2+(y2​−y1​)2​-10-8-6-4-2246810-10-5510A(6.0, 6.0)B(-6.0, -6.0)d = 16.97Delta x = 12Delta y = 12
This is Euclidean Distance.

🔶 Step 4: Assign Nearest Cluster
Each point joins nearest centroid.

🔶 Step 5: Update Centroids
New centroid:
average of all points in cluster.

🔶 Step 6: Repeat
Continue until clusters stop changing.
